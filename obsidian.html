Obsidian

Official Website: obsidian.md

Overview

Obsidian is a powerful, highly customizable Markdown-based personal knowledge management (PKM) application. It operates directly on a local folder of plain text files (known as your vault), providing a future-proof, private, and extremely fast writing and research environment. In this workflow, Obsidian serves as the primary hub for linking atomic ideas, writing literature notes, and compiling research outputs.

Features

  • Local Plain-Text Storage: Notes are stored as standard Markdown files (.md) in your local filesystem, ensuring you always own your data and can open it with any text editor.
  • Internal Links & Graph View: Create connections between notes using wiki-style links ([[Note Name]]). The interactive graph view visualizes the logical structure and relationships within your knowledge base.
  • Extensible Plugin Architecture: Customize your workflow by enabling community-developed plugins (such as Templater, Dataview, and Spaced Repetition).
  • CSS Snippets & Custom Themes: Control the typography and layout of your vault using custom stylesheets to prepare notes for print or screen displays.

Requirements & Installation

  1. Download & Install: Download the installer for your operating system from the official Obsidian website and install it.
  2. Create a Vault: Open the app, select "Create new vault", and choose a directory on your local disk (e.g., /Users/josh/VaultSchar).
  3. Install Workflow Plugins:
    • Go to Settings > Community Plugins and turn community plugins on.
    • Install and configure Zotero Manager, MD Annotation, Multi Exporter, and Equation Library.

Migration

Transitioning to Plain Text: Replaces proprietary, monolithic note databases (like Evernote, Microsoft OneNote, or Apple Notes) with a decentralized, plaintext directory. Your files remain highly portable and resistant to vendor lock-in.
